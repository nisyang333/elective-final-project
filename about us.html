About Us

Our Story

HomeGrown was born from a shared passion for sustainable living and the belief that everyone deserves access to fresh, healthy food. Our journey began in a small community garden, where we witnessed the transformative power of growing our own food. This experience inspired us to create a platform that empowers individuals to cultivate their own gardens, regardless of space or experience.

Our Team

Our team is a diverse group of passionate individuals, including experienced gardeners, nutritionists, and technology experts. We are united by our commitment to making sustainable living accessible and enjoyable for all.

Our Impact

We are dedicated to promoting Health and Well-Being (SDG 3) by providing resources and support for individuals to grow their own food. Our platform has helped thousands of people across the globe cultivate their own gardens, leading to healthier diets and a deeper connection with nature.

Annual Report

We are proud to share our annual report, highlighting the impact we've made in promoting individual health and sustainable living. Our data shows a significant increase in the consumption of fresh produce among our users, leading to improved health outcomes and a greater sense of well-being.
